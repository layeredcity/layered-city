Ramesch Daha, WIEN 1933 – 1935 ⟨https://kunsthallewien.at/en/exhibition/ramesch-daha-wien-1933-1935⟩
Kunsthalle Wien Museumsquartier and Karlsplatz are closed until 11 November due to the installation of our next exhibitions. The artwork by Ramesch Daha for Kunsthalle Wien’s Vitrine can be visited at all times, day or night. Some events will take place during the installation. ‘Lebt und arbeitet in Wien: Postscript’ follows the closure of the exhibition hosting a series of performances and workshops.
Please join us for the exhibition opening of ‘Tiffany Sia: Overt Listening’ ⟨https://kunsthallewien.at/en/event/exhibition-opening-lebt-und-arbeitet-in-wien-contemporary-art-from-vienna⟩  on 12 November at 6pm.
