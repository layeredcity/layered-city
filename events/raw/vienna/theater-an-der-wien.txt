Klangstreich – Eine Note tanzt aus der Reihe FAMILY OPERA FOR YOUNG AND ALL AGED 4 AND ABOVE An adventurous journey through the world of music: two female singers and one male singer tell the story of a musical note that sets off on a journey of discovery, whilst taking the audience on a journey through various musical styles and soundscapes – from Baroque to pop, from jazz to choral music – all without any instruments! Through the power of their voices alone – from singing to beatboxing – the young audience experiences the fascination of music. Der kleine Prinz FAMILY OPERA FOR YOUNG AND ALL AGED 6 AND ABOVE Antoine de Saint-Exupéry’s The Little Prince has been delighting young and all alike for decades with its heartfelt messages. The world of opera has also discovered this wonderful story of responsibility, humanity and friendship. Come and see our family opera, filled with enchanting and romantic music, and join the Little Prince on a unique journey from planet to planet, together with your family and friends. Family ticket: €99 (4 tickets, including at least one person under 16) Get your tickets now! ⟨https://www.theater-wien.at/en/events/season2026-27/1645/Der-kleine-Prinz⟩
Sponsor a student ticket! For many schoolchildren and young people, a visit to the MusikTheater an der Wien is their first encounter with the exciting world of opera – a special experience that opens doors to art and culture. To ensure that as many young people as possible have this opportunity, we need your support: not all young people can afford a ticket (even at a reduced price). When purchasing your tickets online, you have the option to sponsor a student ticket. A student ticket can be funded for as little as 12 euros.
