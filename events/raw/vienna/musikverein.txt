Musikverein Perspektiven: Elfriede Jelinek
The life and literary work of Nobel Prize winner Elfriede Jelinek are deeply influenced by music. Inspired by this, the programme for the Musikverein Perspektiven. It features her musical favourites, music set to her texts, a series of musical and cinematic works by Olga Neuwirth, and, last but not least, recent texts being read in public for the first time. Secure your tickets now for the events featuring Marianne Schroeder, Birgit Minichmayr, Lukas Sternath, Josef Hader and many more!

Lise Davidsen’s stage debut
For the Norwegian soprano Lise Davidsen, who has made a name for herself as an interpreter of Richard Wagner’s heroines, Franz Schubert is also one of the composers to whom she devotes herself with particular passion. The expressive singer is now making a stop at the Musikverein as a lieder singer, accompanied by James Baillieu. Don’t miss her debut on 7 November!
Tickets ⟨https://www.musikverein.at/en/konzert/lise-davidsen-james-baillieu-0006accc/⟩

Season Highlights 26/27
Become a music lover As a supporter, benefactor, maecenas, donor or patron, as well as a regular member of the Gesellschaft der Musikfreunde in Wien and its circle of friends, you enjoy many benefits and help to ensure that the Musikverein continues to exist in its unique form. ⟨https://musikverein.at/en/foerdern/mitgliedschaft/⟩
Artists in focus In the 2026/27 season, we will be honouring Franz Welser-Möst, Anne-Sophie Mutter, Yuja Wang and Maxime Pascal with cycles or focal points at the Musikverein. ⟨https://musikverein.at/en/2026-27/schwerpunkte/kuenstlerinnen-im-fokus/⟩
New Formats Overview of the highlights of the 2026/27 season in the New Formats category ⟨https://musikverein.at/en/2026-27/schwerpunkte/neue-formate/⟩
Composers in Focus In the 2026/27 season, the programme will focus on the two composers Francesca Verunelli and Emilie Mayer. ⟨https://musikverein.at/en/2026-27/schwerpunkte/komponistinnen-im-fokus/⟩
Musikverein for Everyone Participating in cultural life is a human right. The Musikverein takes this mission very seriously and has therefore launched several projects that invite people to concerts who would otherwise have little access to them. ⟨https://musikverein.at/en/2026-27/schwerpunkte/musikverein-fuer-alle/⟩
Themes and Festivals in the 2026/27 season In our themes and festivals in the 2026/27 season, we invite you to rediscover the familiar and hear something new. ⟨https://musikverein.at/en/2026-27/schwerpunkte/themen-und-festivals/⟩
