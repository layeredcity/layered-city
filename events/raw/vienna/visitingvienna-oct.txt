What to do in October
A month like the day before your birthday, when everyone in Vienna stares impatiently into their coffee while waiting for the advent festivities. But October has its own appeal, with, for example, the parks and woods ablaze with autumnal colour.

Top activities in October 2027
Most of Vienna’s tours ⟨https://www.visitingvienna.com/transport/tours-overview/⟩ , concerts ⟨https://www.visitingvienna.com/entertainment/classical-concert/⟩ , museums ⟨https://www.visitingvienna.com/sightseeing/vienna-museums/⟩ and similar remain available all year. But what can October offer in particular?
My quick tip
As we drift from summer to autumn, how about a bit of classical music to ease the transition?
The gorgeous Peterskirche ⟨https://www.visitingvienna.com/sights/peterskirche/⟩ church in the centre hosts evening concerts ⟨https://www.tiqets.com/en/vienna-attractions-c60335/tickets-for-classic-ensemble-vienna-p1008299/?partner=visitingvienna&tq_campaign=LG_Peter⟩ * featuring light classical pieces. The musicians of the Classic Ensemble Vienna perform the works.
Exhibitions
(The Kunsthistorisches Museum always has a super year-end exhibition)
The heavyweight year-end exhibitions have filled museum galleries by October, ready for the coming Christmas visitor rush.
Most schedules are not yet out at the time of writing, but so far I at least know of…
My highlights
The Jewish Museum ⟨https://www.visitingvienna.com/sights/museums/jewish-museum/⟩ explores various aspects and facets of the word resistance in a same-named exhibition, and also tackles myths and more around the role of the Jewish community in the period of creative and intellectual transformation that was Vienna around 1900
One of Austria’s great authors is the subject of a small exhibition at the Literature Museum ⟨https://www.visitingvienna.com/sights/museums/literature-museum/⟩ : Christine Nöstlinger

Special bonuses
Foto Wien ⟨https://www.visitingvienna.com/entertainment/events/foto-wien-festival/⟩ is a biannual event that returns in 2027 to celebrate the medium of photography for a whole month with a huge number of events, exhibitions, and activities (October 1st to 31st)
Gamer heaven & Manga mania
(Only in Vienna: neogothic towers and modern console entertainment)
The huge Game City ⟨https://www.visitingvienna.com/entertainment/events/game-city/⟩ gaming fair plans to return with a fresh concept in 2027 after a one-year break (details and dates TBA).
Previous editions occupied the Rathausplatz square and featured eSports events, cosplay, merchandise and numerous stands showcasing, for example, the latest games and gaming hardware.
Entry was free, and a visit also got you a look at the rather gorgeous looking halls and staircases of the Rathaus (city hall).
Meanwhile, the AniMarkt ⟨https://animarket.animanga.at/⟩ market with a strong manga and anime theme has its autumn edition (usually October 26th but 2027 dates TBA). Lots of fan art and pop culture creations up for sale.
Museums, but not as you know them
(Numerous locations participate)
This is a fun event.
A whole bunch of Vienna’s museums throw open their doors from 6pm until midnight and usher you in to experience special tours, displays, and/or entertainment one evening in October.
A lovely buzz runs through the city during this Long Night of Museums ⟨https://www.visitingvienna.com/entertainment/events/night-of-the-museums/⟩ (2027 dates TBA), which the ORF state broadcasting company organises. Thousands mill the streets with a single ticket covering entry to any participating museum.
The silver screen
(The Gartenbau cinema is a film festival mainstay)
A notable October highlight will appeal to fans of cinema.
Vienna’s leading film festival, the long-running Viennale ⟨https://www.visitingvienna.com/entertainment/events/viennale-film-festival/⟩ , has all the accoutrements you expect of such an event: prestigious prizes, special guests, national and international pre-release films, and more (2027 dates TBA).
One further popular film festival for you: the SLASH film festival ⟨https://www.visitingvienna.com/entertainment/events/slash-film-festival/⟩ continues through into early October (2027 dates TBA).

Form, function, fun, food (and drink)
(Look for Edelstoff in the Marx Halle)
October also traditionally hosts some special art, craft & design events. For example:
Vienna Design Week ⟨https://www.visitingvienna.com/entertainment/events/vienna-design-week/⟩ (2027 dates TBA) has numerous exhibitions and other events across the theme
The lovely Edelstoff ⟨https://www.visitingvienna.com/entertainment/events/edelstoff/⟩ design market visits the Marx Halle for its autumn edition (2027 dates TBA)
FAIR FOR ART Vienna ⟨https://www.fairforart-vienna.at/⟩ is a fair for modern and contemporary art that takes placed in the Künstlerhaus ⟨https://www.visitingvienna.com/sights/museums/kuenstlerhaus/⟩ (2027 dates TBA)
The Vienna Highlights art fair ⟨https://www.highlights.info/⟩ (2027 dates TBA): this invitation-only event brings art into the hallowed halls of Palais Esterházy
After all that art and design, you might need to settle into an armchair, pour yourself a drink or make yourself a sandwich, and recharge your batteries. Alternatively:
Drop into the GENUSSWELTEN ⟨https://genusswelten.at/⟩ gourmet festival at Palais Auersperg ⟨https://www.visitingvienna.com/entertainment/palais-auersperg/⟩ (2027 dates TBA)
Pop by the MIT ALLES Kulinarikfest ⟨https://mitalles.xyz/⟩ culinary festival in the Projektraum WUK (next dates TBA)
Take a sip or two of tea at the Vienna Tea Festival ⟨https://www.viennateafestival.at/⟩ in the Reaktor venue (2027 dates TBA)
Parades and displays
October 26th is Austria’s official national holiday. So you may find parades and other events around the town centre.
The Austrian army typically puts on one or more displays on Heldenplatz ⟨https://www.visitingvienna.com/sights/winter-palace/heldenplatz/⟩ and elsewhere, while the emergency services and other public safety organisations present their work on the big Rathausplatz ⟨https://www.visitingvienna.com/entertainment/rathaus/⟩ square in front of town hall at the Wiener Sicherheitsfest ⟨https://diehelferwiens.wien.gv.at/wiener-sicherheitsfest⟩ (2027 dates TBA).
Music & merriment
(Get ready to, um, rock?)
There’s no better time to hold an Oktoberfest than, um, in October.
A Vienna equivalent was the former Wiener Wiesn Fest ⟨https://www.visitingvienna.com/entertainment/events/wiener-wiesn/⟩ , but the new Kaiser Wiesn event ⟨https://kaiserwiesn.at/⟩ started up at a similar location and runs from September 23rd until October 10th in 2027. So pack your Lederhosen, gentlemen and your Dirndls, ladies. There will be much feasting and music.
If you prefer electric guitars and synthesisers to Oktoberfest accordions, then take in:
Waves Vienna ⟨https://www.visitingvienna.com/entertainment/events/waves-vienna/⟩ (October 7th to 9th), which showcases new musical talent from around the world through dozens of live performances. And there’s a side programme of events for those wishing to dig deeper into the current issues facing the music industry
We may also yet get to see a revival and return of the Indie Label Woche ⟨https://www.visitingvienna.com/entertainment/events/indie-label-week/⟩ (next dates TBA). The Reigen music venue hosts rock to pop to punk to hip hop (and more) across six nights of gigs showcasing local talent
Top tennis
An ATP500 tennis tournament often reaches Vienna with the Erste Bank Open ⟨https://www.erstebank-open.com/en⟩ (next dates TBA).
Seasonal tip…
Enjoy the autumn colours
(Spotted on a woodland walk)
Autumn turns the city’s trees into swirls of yellows and reds. And we have a lot of trees. For some seasonal ambience, consider:
The Vienna woods: these dominate the western edge of the city, but you needn’t travel far from the centre to enjoy nature’s transformation. Try, for example, the Stadtpark ⟨https://www.visitingvienna.com/sights/stadtpark/⟩ , where the Strauss statue adds another golden hue to October’s colours
Continue the autumnal feeling at the Wiener Herbsttage ⟨https://www.visitingvienna.com/entertainment/events/wiener-herbsttage/⟩ Viennese Autumn Days (October 9th to 11th) in the palm house and surrounds of the Burggarten park. Expect rare fruit varieties, arts, crafts, and edibles
Or pop down to the Prater ⟨https://www.visitingvienna.com/entertainment/prater/⟩ to combine a ferris wheel ride ⟨https://www.visitingvienna.com/sights/riesenrad/⟩ with a long country walk along hiking trail 9 ⟨https://www.visitingvienna.com/entertainment/prater-park-hiking-trail-9/⟩
And if things turn a little chilly later in the day in October, how about some ideas for evening activities ⟨https://www.visitingvienna.com/entertainment/evening/⟩ ?
