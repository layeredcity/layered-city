What to do in October
A month like the day before your birthday, when everyone in Vienna stares impatiently into their coffee while waiting for the advent festivities. But October has its own appeal, with, for example, the parks and woods ablaze with autumnal colour.

Top activities in October 2026
Most of Vienna’s tours , concerts , museums and similar remain available all year. But what can October offer in particular?
My quick tip
As we drift from summer to autumn, how about a bit of classical music to ease the transition?
The gorgeous Peterskirche church in the centre hosts evening concerts * featuring light classical pieces. The musicians of the Classic Ensemble Vienna perform the works.
Exhibitions
(The Kunsthistorisches Museum always has a super year-end exhibition)
The heavyweight year-end exhibitions have filled museum galleries by October, ready for the coming Christmas visitor rush.
My highlights
The Albertina’s big winter exhibition builds a dialogue between works by Picasso and Bacon. Also catch a solo exhibition for Shara Hughes (from October 9th), a broader look at women artists through history that draws on the Albertina’s own collections (from October 30th), and Otto Waalkes’s reimagining of Albertina masterpieces
It’s your last chance to see both the Albertina’s 250th anniversary exhibition that includes a rare appearance for Dürer’s famous Young Hare watercolour (until October 11th) and their exhibition of travel photography from the late 1800s (until October 26th)
Enjoy the last days (until October 11th) of Upper Belvedere’s in-depth look at two paintings by Johann Baptist Lampi the Elder and the Younger
Two solo exhibitions continue at Lower Belvedere. One for a prominent name from the post-WWII avant garde landscape in Poland: Erna Rosenstein. And one for an artist, educator and cornerstone of Viennese Kineticism: Erika Giovanna Klien
Over at the Albertina Modern , enjoy their exhibition featuring the masters of motion Victor Vasarely and Marc Adrian
We have the final days of the Premiere! exhibition at the Leopold Museum with highlights from the modern and contemporary art collection of the Austrian National Bank (until October 11th). But they continue with an exhibition of works by Herbert Boeckl and Hans Josephsohn
And the major exhibition of contemporary art from Vienna comes to a close at the Kunsthalle Wien (runs until October 26th)
Feel the tension and interplay between avant garde and academic approaches to art at the Academy of Fine Arts Vienna exhibition
The Jewish Museum brings us the often humorous paintings of Eran Shakine and a biographical look at the lives of those driven into exile during Austria’s Nazi years
Discover the art of Elisabeth von Samsonow in a solo exhibition at the Heidi Horten Collection , but also (from October 20th) peruse artistic positions with the motif of the woman’s shoe
Ad:
Rarely-exhibited items across three themes appear in the Weltmuseum’s Hidden Stories . They also take an introspective look at weapons and the historical arms trade in a collection context. And build a museal bridge to the Ishír indigenous people of western Paraguay
The relationship between animals and humans as portrayed in art through the centuries appears in the latest exhibition at the Dom Museum
Discover the meaning of colours at the Wien Museum, as told through four centuries of Viennese fashion
From fashion to design…the Furniture Museum reveals how Austrian furniture design developed in the aftermath of WWII
Two of Austria’s great authors are the subject of exhibitions at the Literature Museum : Thomas Bernhard and (from October 8th) Christine Nöstlinger
Get informed on the issues around resource consumption, consumer choices, sustainability and the circular economy at the Technisches Museum’s More than Recycling exhibition
More issues to ponder at the Naturhistorisches Museum, which takes a critical look at its own past , especially the acquisition history of the collection. And learn about the lives of early medieval folk with the GENchangers exhibition (from October 14th)
Travel further back in time to explore political machinations and murder in the ancient world at the Kunsthistorisches Museum (from October 20th and hosted in the Neue Burg )
Staying with historical journeys…the National Library presents love through the centuries in its wonderful State Hall , while its Bridge of Humanity photo exhibition (from October 14th) looks back at the topic of Austria and the 1956 uprising in Hungary
And another trip through time…the MuseumsQuartier celebrates its silver anniversary with an exhibition on the location’s long history and the modern complex’s origin story
See also the wider exhibition listings for more suggestions and details as they arrive…
Special bonuses
Foto Wien is a biannual event that celebrates the medium of photography for a whole month with a huge number of events, exhibitions, and activities (next dates 2027, though)
Gamer heaven & Manga mania
(Only in Vienna: neogothic towers and modern console entertainment)
The huge Game City gaming fair (skips 2026 and returns with a fresh concept in 2027) occupies the Rathausplatz square and features eSports events, cosplay, merchandise and numerous stands showcasing, for example, the latest games and gaming hardware. Entry is free.
A visit also gets you a look at the rather gorgeous looking halls and staircases of the Rathaus (city hall).
Meanwhile, the AniMarkt market with a strong manga and anime theme has its autumn edition (October 26th). Lots of fan art and pop culture creations up for sale.
Museums, but not as you know them
(Numerous locations participate)
This is a fun event.
A whole bunch of Vienna’s museums throw open their doors from 6pm until midnight and usher you in to experience special tours, displays, and/or entertainment one evening in October.
A lovely buzz runs through the city during this Long Night of Museums (October 3rd), which the ORF state broadcasting company organises. Thousands mill the streets with a single ticket covering entry to any participating museum.
The silver screen
(The Gartenbau cinema is a film festival mainstay)
A notable October highlight will appeal to fans of cinema.
Vienna’s leading film festival, the long-running Viennale , has all the accoutrements you expect of such an event: prestigious prizes, special guests, national and international pre-release films, and more (October 22nd to November 3rd).
One further film festival: the SLASH film festival continues through into early October in 2026 (September 23rd to October 4th)

Form, function, fun, food (and drink)
(Look for Edelstoff in the Marx Halle)
October also traditionally hosts some special art, craft & design events. For example:
Vienna Design Week (September 25th to October 4th) has numerous exhibitions and other events across the theme
The lovely Edelstoff design market visits the Marx Halle for its autumn edition (October 3rd and 4th)
FAIR FOR ART Vienna is a fair for modern and contemporary art that takes placed in the Künstlerhaus (October 7th to 11th)
The Vienna Highlights art fair (October 1st to 4th): this invitation-only event brings art into the hallowed halls of Palais Esterházy
After all that art and design, you might need to settle into an armchair, pour yourself a drink or make yourself a sandwich, and recharge your batteries. Alternatively:
Drop into the GENUSSWELTEN gourmet festival at Palais Auersperg (October 16th to 18th)
Pop by the MIT ALLES Kulinarikfest culinary festival in the Projektraum WUK (next dates TBA)
Take a sip or two of tea at the Vienna Tea Festival in the Reaktor venue (October 3rd to 4th)
Parades and displays
October 26th is Austria’s official national holiday. So you may find parades and other events around the town centre.
The Austrian army typically puts on one or more displays on Heldenplatz and elsewhere, while the emergency services and other public safety organisations present their work on the big Rathausplatz square in front of town hall at the Wiener Sicherheitsfest (October 25th and 26th).
Music & merriment
(Get ready to, um, rock?)
There’s no better time to hold an Oktoberfest than, um, in October.
A Vienna equivalent was the former Wiener Wiesn Fest , but the new Kaiser Wiesn event started up at a similar location and runs from September 24th until October 11th in 2026. So pack your Lederhosen, gentlemen and your Dirndls, ladies. There will be much feasting and music.
If you prefer electric guitars and synthesisers to Oktoberfest accordions, then take in:
Waves Vienna (October 1st to 3rd), which showcases new musical talent from around the world through dozens of live performances. And there’s a side programme of events for those wishing to dig deeper into the current issues facing the music industry
We may also yet get to see a revival and return of the Indie Label Woche (next dates TBA). The Reigen music venue hosts rock to pop to punk to hip hop (and more) across six nights of gigs showcasing local talent
Top tennis
An ATP500 tennis tournament reaches Vienna with the Erste Bank Open (October 24th to November 1st). Top seed Jannik Sinner justified his status by beating Alexander Zverev in the final last time out.
Seasonal tip…
Enjoy the autumn colours
(Spotted on a woodland walk)
Autumn turns the city’s trees into swirls of yellows and reds. And we have a lot of trees. For some seasonal ambience, consider:
The Vienna woods: these dominate the western edge of the city, but you needn’t travel far from the centre to enjoy nature’s transformation. Try, for example, the Stadtpark , where the Strauss statue adds another golden hue to October’s colours
Continue the autumnal feeling at the Wiener Herbsttage Viennese Autumn Days (October 9th to 11th) in the palm house and surrounds of the Burggarten park. Expect rare fruit varieties, arts, crafts, and edibles
Or pop down to the Prater to combine a ferris wheel ride with a long country walk along hiking trail 9
