VIENNA ART WEEK 2026
NAVIGATING ANGST
How does art guide us through the treacherous waters of the present? How do we guide art through the shallows of political turmoil?
With this year’s theme, NAVIGATING ANGST, VIENNA ART WEEK 2026 focuses on an experience that is omnipresent yet rarely articulated with precision: angst.
VIENNA ART WEEK takes the present, with all its contradictions, seriously and invites us to engage with it without letting it overwhelm us. Perhaps this is precisely where a form of orientation lies: not in reassurance, but in the ability to endure complexity — and to remain capable of acting within it.
Program ⟨https://www.viennaartweek.at/en/vienna-art-week-2026/⟩
Every year, VIENNA ART WEEK shines a spotlight on Vienna’s vibrant visual arts scene. Together with around 70 program partners, the festival offers a week of inspiring art from the present to the Baroque with free admission.
VIENNA ART WEEK sees itself as a joint platform of the Viennese art community and is based on a cooperation of Viennese art institutions, art universities, galleries, independent art spaces, and artists that is unique worldwide.

A Look Back at
VIENNA ART WEEK 2025
The 21st edition of VIENNA ART WEEK centered on learning—not as the simple accumulation of knowledge, but as an ongoing process of recognition, unlearning, and rethinking.
The theme LEARNING SYSTEMS invited us to reflect on the structures that shape how knowledge is formed, transmitted, and transformed—from inherited traditions to emerging technologies—and to explore new perspectives collectively.
