Ecosystems for Listening
On opening day, Wien Modern 2026 starts with unexpected sounds by grasshoppers, Japanese leafhoppers, nursery web spiders, wood ants, psyllids, Anopheles mosquitoes and mason bees. On the first few days of the festival, breathtakingly detailed laser microphone sound recordings will be made audible by the Swiss Chuchchepati Orchestra in the foyer of the Wiener Konzerthaus – through large loudspeakers from Chuchchepati, a district of Kathmandu in Nepal. But insects aren’t even the smallest among the many living creatures which are brought into focus by the numerous artists contributing to the 39th edition of Wien Modern.

FESTIVAL
New music for the city
With around a month of events at countless locations all over the city , Wien Modern is the biggest festival for contemporary arts music in Austria and one of the biggest of its kind worldwide. It was founded in 1988 at the initiative of Claudio Abbado, conductor and the general music director of the city of Vienna at the time. Since then, it takes place every year in November in Vienna.
Exactly what is to be expected from "contemporary arts music" and from a festival with the name "Wien Modern", which is at odds with all the clichés of Vienna?
