Flea markets
Discover unique treasures
In Berlin, you can stroll through different colourful, funky and creative flea markets every day. Hunt for antiques, art and a piece of GDR culture at the market on Boxhagener Platz ⟨https://www.visitberlin.de/en/flea-market-boxhagener-platz⟩ . In front of Schöneberg Town Hall ⟨https://www.visitberlin.de/en/flea-market-rathaus-schoneberg⟩ , where John F. Kennedy once gave his famous speech, books, household goods, toys and clothing are on offer on Saturdays and Sundays. The antique market at Ostbahnhof ⟨https://www.visitberlin.de/en/grosser-antikmarkt-am-ostbahnhof⟩ is particularly well known for old postcards and coins. On Straße des 17. Juni, you can browse for art and souvenirs at Berlin's oldest and largest flea market ⟨https://www.visitberlin.de/en/kunstmarkt-strasse-des-17-juni⟩ .
The market in Mauerpark ⟨https://www.visitberlin.de/en/flea-market-mauerpark⟩ is also well known. Every Sunday, it offers an unusual assortment of goods, culinary delights from around the world and music. For vintage clothing, the flea markets at Marheinekeplatz, Maybach Ufer ⟨https://www.visitberlin.de/en/neukollner-wochenmarkte-am-maybachufer⟩ and Arkonaplatz ⟨https://www.visitberlin.de/en/flea-market-arkonaplatz⟩ are the best choice.

&copy; visitBerlin, Foto: Dagmar Schwelle Flea market on Boxhagener Platz Flea market on Boxhagener Platz Flea market on Boxhagener Platz Every Sunday a large flea market is held on the Boxhagener Platz. Located in the heart of the vibrant student quarter around Simon-Dach-Strasse in Friedrichshain, it is a wonderful place to stroll and rummage around. You can find everything here, from books and furniture to household equipment and fashion. The stands are arranged around the square. There are numerous restaurants and snacks in the immediate vicinity of the market, while the meadow in the square’s centre is a great place to chill out after shopping. The atmosphere here is relaxed and calm. Many visitors mainly come to talk shop or just look around in amazement. Many professional dealers also mingle here. Since it is hard to guarantee whether items in a flea market are genuine, an experienced eye is a definite advantage. The market is on Erich-Steinfurth-Strasse, every Sunday from 9 am to 5 pm.
