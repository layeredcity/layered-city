Flea markets
Discover unique treasures
In Berlin, you can stroll through different colourful, funky and creative flea markets every day. Hunt for antiques, art and a piece of GDR culture at the market on Boxhagener Platz ⟨https://www.visitberlin.de/en/flea-market-boxhagener-platz⟩ . In front of Schöneberg Town Hall ⟨https://www.visitberlin.de/en/flea-market-rathaus-schoneberg⟩ , where John F. Kennedy once gave his famous speech, books, household goods, toys and clothing are on offer on Saturdays and Sundays. The antique market at Ostbahnhof ⟨https://www.visitberlin.de/en/grosser-antikmarkt-am-ostbahnhof⟩ is particularly well known for old postcards and coins. On Straße des 17. Juni, you can browse for art and souvenirs at Berlin's oldest and largest flea market ⟨https://www.visitberlin.de/en/kunstmarkt-strasse-des-17-juni⟩ .
The market in Mauerpark ⟨https://www.visitberlin.de/en/flea-market-mauerpark⟩ is also well known. Every Sunday, it offers an unusual assortment of goods, culinary delights from around the world and music. For vintage clothing, the flea markets at Marheinekeplatz, Maybach Ufer ⟨https://www.visitberlin.de/en/neukollner-wochenmarkte-am-maybachufer⟩ and Arkonaplatz ⟨https://www.visitberlin.de/en/flea-market-arkonaplatz⟩ are the best choice.

&copy; visitBerlin, Foto: Dagmar Schwelle Flea market on Boxhagener Platz Every Sunday a large flea market is held on the Boxhagener Platz. Located in the heart of the vibrant student quarter around Simon-Dach-Strasse in Friedrichshain, it is a wonderful place to stroll and rummage around. You can find everything here, from books and furniture to household equipment and fashion. The stands are arranged around the square. There are numerous restaurants and snacks in the immediate vicinity of the market, while the meadow in the square’s centre is a great place to chill out after shopping. Sunday 10am to 6pm Boxhagener Platz, 10245 Berlin ⟨https://www.visitberlin.de/en/flea-market-boxhagener-platz⟩
&copy; oldthing.de Großer Antikmarkt am Ostbahnhof Anyone looking for antiques and old furniture, or records and books, will find themselves drawn to the great antiques market at Ostbahnhof. The atmosphere here is relaxed and calm. Many visitors mainly come to talk shop or just look around in amazement. Many professional dealers also mingle here. Since it is hard to guarantee whether items in a flea market are genuine, an experienced eye is a definite advantage. The market is on Erich-Steinfurth-Strasse, every Sunday from 9 am to 5 pm. The vendors change constantly, bringing new goods from all over the world to the city. Sunday 9am to 5pm Erich-Steinfurth-Straße 1, 10243 Berlin ⟨https://www.visitberlin.de/en/grosser-antikmarkt-am-ostbahnhof⟩

&copy; Getty Images, Foto: Dominic Ruefenacht/EyeEm Antiques and Book Market near the Bodemuseum The Antiques and Book Market near the Bodemuseum is located on the Museum Island, well removed from the busy crowds on the prestigious Unter den Linden. The market is an ideal place for a relaxed 'snoop-around' if you want to take a break from sightseeing. Around 60 stands every Saturday and Sunday offer books, records, CDs, handicrafts and furniture.
