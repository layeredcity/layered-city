Classic music in Berlin
Komische Oper Berlin &copy; visitBerlin, Foto: Wolfgang Scholvien
The most beautiful concerts and operas
Enjoy top-class productions at Berlin's opera houses ⟨https://www.visitberlin.de/en/category/opera-operetta⟩ and performances by world-class orchestras. Three major opera houses and seven large orchestras weave a tapestry of sound in Berlin that is second to none. Great conductors such as Petrenko, Thielemann, Runnicles and Gaffigan showcase their talents and will captivate you too. Staged productions of well-known works and performances allow you to discover new worlds of sound – and experience old favourites in a new light.
Why not listen to our Berlin Unboxed podcast on Berlin as a city of classical music ⟨https://www.visitberlin.de/de/berlin-unboxed-der-visitberlin-podcast#a3⟩ in advance?
