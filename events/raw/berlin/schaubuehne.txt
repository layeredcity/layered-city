Season Campaign 2026/27 by TOILETPAPER ⟨https://www.schaubuehne.de/en/start/news/spielzeitkampagne-202627-von-toiletpaper.html⟩
This season’s publicity campaign has been created by TOILETPAPER. Since 2010, Italian artist Maurizio Cattelan and photographer Pierpaolo Ferrari have been publishing the eponymous magazine which consists entirely of staged photographs. For the Schaubühne, they have put our ensemble centre stage, portraying each actor in a bespoke way. The backdrops and styling were realised by the Schaubühne’s production departments of set design, costumes, props and hair and makeup.  You can find the pictures of the campaign in the season booklet of the Schaubühne (to be seen hier ⟨https://www.schaubuehne.de/en/start/uploads/Spielzeitheft/Spielzeitheft_1-_Haelfte_202627.pdf⟩ as PDF) and spread over the season as large billboards in the urban area of ​​ Berlin.
First Half of the Season 2026/27 ⟨https://www.schaubuehne.de/en/start/news/1-haelfte-der-spielzeit-202627.html⟩
We are pleased to present this booklet, offering an overview of the premieres and programme through February 2027. The season brochure is now available in the building and can also be read online here ⟨https://www.schaubuehne.de/en/start/uploads/Spielzeitheft_1-_Haelfte_202627.pdf⟩ .
