What’s On This Week: Our Daily Tips
A curated selection of the best things to do daily in Berlin.
MON 23
Chelsea Wolfe – The Dark  World Tour
Photo credit: Imago/Martin Müller
Following the release of her ninth studio album, The Dark , in August, Chelsea Wolfe brings her world tour to Berlin. Blending goth rock, doom metal and folk, she serves up a brooding sound for her devoted fanbase. If you’ve yet to experience this contemporary goth icon live, expect a dark, dreamy stage show that’s well worth sinking into.

TUE 24
Tipsy Gay Tuesdays
Photo credit: Emmanuele Contini
Friends of Dorothy now have their own home, and one thing’s for certain – there’s lot’s of balls. Bingo balls, that is. Although this night is tailored towards homos, gay boys and queer men, it’s worth noting that Tipsy Bear is open to all. Games begin around 8pm. Eye’s down and hope for a 69. Bingo!
Tipsy Bear , doors 18:00 details ⟨https://www.eventbrite.de/e/tipsy-gay-tuesdays-tickets-2002492741696⟩
WED 25
Soundwatch Music Film Festival #10
Photo credit: Imago/Martin Müller
With a programme packed full of trailblazers, it’s hard to know where to start. Back for its 10th edition, the festival dedicated to sound and vision, features films that include Marianne Faithfull, Peaches, William Burroughs and Sonic Youth. So if punk and subcultural renegades are your jam, this is an absolute gem. Through Nov 29.
Lichtblick Kino , starts 19:00 details ⟨https://lichtblick-kino.org/reihe/26-11-soundwatch-10/⟩
THU 26
Around the World in 14 Films
Photo credit: Still from ‘La Gradiva’, director Marine Atlan, Les Films du Poisson, BiBi Film, Centre national du cinéma et de l’image animée (CNC)
Your watchlist just got a serious update. Presenting 14 different films from 15 different countries, Around the World in 14 Films brings its top picks to Berlin. Each choice tells a unique story from around the globe that might otherwise slip through the cracks before reaching German screens. The screenings will take place across three cinemas in three different neighborhoods and the festival concludes with a jury prize for best director. Through Dec 6.
Various venues , details ⟨https://www.14films.de/⟩
FRI 27
Disco Tehran
Photo credit: SaHHar
All the way from New York, Disco Tehran is making their way to Berlin for another memorable night. This multicultural collective is all about bringing 1970 Tehran discotheque to the dance floor, where one can dance to groovy throwbacks and rare global music finds. The night is joined together with Berlin-based Sahhara collective, scheduled to take over the second room because one dance floor is hardly enough to contain the immaculate vibes.
Gretchen, doors 22:00 details ⟨https://ra.co/events/2534951⟩
SAT 28
Protect The Dolls: A Casa Kuà Fundraiser
Photo credit: Zuma Press Wire
Following the raid at Casa Kuà several weeks ago and the complicated support for trans healthcare in Germany, The Dolls are throwing a daytime fundraiser for the queer health centre and the wider trans community. A show of solidarity from allies, the party is headlined by scene icons Butchqueen.fm, elva, Organza and Posture, with food supplied by All Blue Kitchen.
Crack Bellmer, doors 13:00 details ⟨https://ra.co/events/2584814⟩
SUN 29
Autumn Run
Photo credit: Imago/FotoFunkeServices
The annual Berlin forest run or Herbstwaldlauf is back, and you can drag your family and friends along with you. This marathon also offers an 11km run and other various shorter distances for those who don’t necessarily want to suffer. All participants who conclude the marathon get a medal, and if that isn’t rewarding enough, Christmas carbs like Stollen and tea will be served at the finish line to celebrate the forest run landing on the first Advent.
Sportplatz Heidenheimer Straße , starts 10:00 details ⟨https://herbstwaldlauf-berlin.de/⟩
