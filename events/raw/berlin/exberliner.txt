What’s On This Week: Our Daily Tips
A curated selection of the best things to do daily in Berlin.
MON 09
AFRIKAMERA – Current Cinema from Africa
Photo Credit: Afrotonix by Bernadette
Highlighting the key social and political issues facing the African continent today and in the future, this film festival focuses on a selection of feature films, documentaries and short films. Under the theme ‘Reflect’, its programme explores colonialism, climate change and exploitation and includes an exhibition and networking events too. Through Nov 15

TUE 10
Italian Film Festival
Photo credit: Italian Film Festival Berlin still from The Last One for the Road (Le città di pianura) by Francesco Sossai
There’s little Italians do badly, with film certainly being one of their fortes. The 13th edition of this festival showcases a selection of Italian films from the 2025/26 cinema season with most of them presented by their directors, actors or other participants. It makes for a unique slice of Italian culture here in Berlin. Through Nov 14.
CineStar (Kino in der KulturBrauerei) , details ⟨https://www.italianfilmfestivalberlin.com/⟩
WED 11
Interpol and Bloc Party
Photo credit: Imago/Zuma Press
We recently interviewed Interpol frontman Paul Banks about the band’s new album, so if you were inspired, now’s your chance to hear it IRL. Co-headlining the tour are fellow titans of the early-00s indie scene, Bloc Party. Time to dust off those skinny jeans and relive the glory days.
Uber Arena , doors 18:00 details ⟨https://www.uber-arena.de/events/detail/bloc-party-interpol⟩
THU 12
Drone Diaries
Photo credit: Drone Diaries
What if the systems tracking our every move turned against themselves? In Drone Diaries , five performers transform CANK’s second floor into a live surveillance drama, drawing on everything from AI and data harvesting to the Stasi’s methods of monitoring citizens. As the digital agents begin to challenge their own programming, this high-energy dance performance asks how much of our privacy we’re prepared to give up and whether we can still take back control.
CANK , starts 19:00 , details ⟨http://www.eventfrog.de/dronediaries⟩
FRI 13
Soft Squishy Things
Photo credit: Kendra Epik Photography
Object theatre aficionado Robin Leveroos performs her one-woman show, which you could say is out of this world. The work explores themes of loneliness, identity and connection from inside a stranded astronaut’s abode. If you’ve ever wanted to watch a ventilator become a hype squad, now’s the time. Through Nov 15.
Schaubude, starts 20:00 details ⟨https://schaubude.berlin/en/program/soft-squishy-things/⟩
SAT 14
20 Years of F-Cat Festival
Photo credit: Makar Artemev
Get ready for a FAT lineup of acts to celebrate 20 years of Berlin-based international music booking agency and tour promoters, F-Cat. To commemorate this milestone, they’ve pulled in the likes of DELUXE, Ziggy Zeitgeist, Rasha Nahas and Smag Pa Dig Selv. Mixing global pop, jazz, soul and electronic music, it showcases the diverse range of acts and music they love.
Festsaal Kreuzberg , doors 18:00 details ⟨https://festsaal-kreuzberg.de/de/programm/20-Years-F-Cat-Festival⟩
SUN 15
30 Years of Hamburger Bahnhof
Photo credit: Hamburger Bahnhof
On November 3, 1996, Hamburger Bahnhof opened its doors as Berlin’s Museum of Contemporary Art. 30 years later, it’s still at the forefront of Berlin’s art scene. To celebrate this milestone, they’re opening for a full 30 hours over the weekend with continuous guided tours, late night performances, pyjama walks, tents for chillin’, film screenings and more. Plus it’s all for free.
Hamburger Bahnhof . Running all day details ⟨https://www.smb.museum/museen-einrichtungen/hamburger-bahnhof/ueber-uns/30-jahre-hamburger-bahnhof/⟩
