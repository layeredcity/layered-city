What’s On This Week: Our Daily Tips
A curated selection of the best things to do daily in Berlin.
MON 02
Experimontag
Photo Credit: Giacomo Bianco
This weekly Berlin institution (we think it’s fair to say) is a mecca for audiophiles and experimental heads. Each week, three artists bring something different, with ever more inventive ways to produce sound. On this occasion the performers are [o1 Temp], Matto Zoppi, Francesco Toninelli and a DJ-set from Jurassique Parque.

Madame Claude , doors 19:30 details ⟨https://madameclaude.de/event/o1-temp-matto-zoppi-francesco-toninelli-jurassique-parque-dj-set/⟩
TUE 03
Most Wanted: Music
Photo credit:Stimulus by Anna Vis
Shaping the future of Berlin’s music scene, this covention-cum-festival-cum-awards-show is a huge showcase of emerging musical talent in the capital. It features panel talks by leading industry figures and aims to give insights on how to cut your teeth as a future superstar. Through Nov 5.
Various locations , details ⟨https://mwm-berlin.de/⟩
WED 04
Bazaar Berlin
Photo credit: Bazaar Berlin
If you’re looking to get a head start on your Christmas shopping, this huge extravaganza is like browsing the souks of Istanbul or the backstreets of Mexico City. With more than 500 manufacturers and retailers from over 60 countries, it’s a one-stop-shop for everything from global curiosities to unique gifts. This year, they’ve added fossils, minerals and precious gems to the mix. Through Nov 8.
Messe Berlin , doors 10:00 details ⟨https://www.bazaar-berlin.de/de/besuchen/tickets?gad_source=1&gad_campaignid=24300454031&gclid=Cj0KCQjw3YvXBhCDARIsAKB-ox86wCx77tVfkNocMRyZPw9r4cXX-Wm0DgB_Rq-ufov9UHbNzg9eAjoaAnoIEALw_wcB⟩
THU 05
Punk & Poetry
Photo credit: Nics Stage
It seems like ‘the last analogue decade’, AKA the 90s, is having a renaissance. Nics Stage has been shooting the likes of Nick Cave, Courtney Love, Skin, circus performers and punk gigs from the perspective of the mosh pit or the side of the stage. Now they’re having an exhibition of their actual photographs ranging from 1994-2026. Through Dec 6.
Pirata Patata , doors 18:00 , details ⟨https://www.instagram.com/p/DeRqv7EMWbu/?hl=en⟩
FRI 06
Berlin Gaming Festival
Photo credit: Berlin Gaming Festival
Gaming is one of Europe’s fastest growing industries. This festival kicks it up a notch and adds a cosplay costume competition, never-before-seen game content, workshops, TCG, board games, the LAN, XR and the indie exhibition. It’s a must for the gaming community and anyone who wants to re-discover their childhood favs. Through Nov 8.
Alte Münze , doors 10:00 details ⟨https://www.gamesground.de/de⟩
SAT 07
Ink and Art
Bringing edge back to exhibitions, this vernissage is a collection of more than 40 artists alongside tattooists, musicians, piercing and tooth gems stations, all housed within MaHalla’s Black Hall. The idea behind Ink and Art is to fight back against the loss of subcultures and their spaces by uniting all mediums in one room.
MaHalla , doors 15:00 details ⟨https://www.mahalla.berlin/program/ink-art⟩
SUN 08
The Drag Festival Berlin Winter Edition
Photo credit: Matty Parks Photography
You better work! That Gay Creation is hosting another drag explosion that’ll set you up for a very queer winter. Hosted by Miss Ivanka T, alongside appearances from Britney Manson, Lolo Limonero and a host of exquisite queens and kings of the Berlin scene. Plus there’s a big queer market and a lesbian pie-eating competition. Now, sashay away.
Festsaal Kreuzberg, doors 12:00 details ⟨https://festsaal-kreuzberg.de/de/programm/the-drag-festival-berlin⟩
