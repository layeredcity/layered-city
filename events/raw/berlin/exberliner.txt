What’s On This Week: Our Daily Tips
A curated selection of the best things to do daily in Berlin.
MON 26
Jazzfest Berlin
Photo Credit: Sheen Trio: Transitory
Dust off your fedoras, Jazzfest is back. The festival will host more than 135 of the world’s finest and funkiest musicians. Alongside the vast lineup of musical events, there will be a number of film screenings, one of which is an Oscar nominee. The festival will conclude with a Berlin legend: Alexander von Schlippenbach and his Globe Unity Orchestra. The ensemble made music history in the 1960s when it challenged audiences at the Philharmonie Berlin with its uncompromising free jazz.

TUE 27
What is Love? Standup English Comedy
Photo credit: Nya Yeanafehn
Swapping heartbreak for belly laughs, this English-language comedy night mines the highs, lows, confessions and intimate mishaps of modern love. Hosted by local legend Nya Yeanafehn, with some of Berlin’s funniest comedians and a few surprise guests, it’s the perfect cure for a dating disaster.
SaliGari Bar s , details ⟨https://fienta.com/what-is-love-196864?utm_source=refber&utm_medium=link&utm_campaign=wil⟩
WED 28
Sound Metaphors Festival Part 2
Photo credit: Mak Artemev
If you’re craving techno, ambient, dub and live electronic music, the Sound Metaphors Festival has you covered. Back for part two and located in the big house itself, the carefully selected acts focus primarily on live performances, human expression and a deep love for music. It’s on through Oct 30.
Kantine am Berghain, doors 19:00 details ⟨https://ra.co/events/2528461⟩
THU 29
House of Lunacy: Danse Macabre
Photo credit: Roni Lugassi
Slowly, the leaves are falling from the trees and the year is drawing to a close. House of Lunacy, too, has reached the end of its cycle with Chapter IV: Decay. Inspired by the iconic Anita Berber and the scandalous salons of Montmartre, it’s all about corsets, torn lace, androgynous suits and opulent costumes, colliding with electronic beats, theatre and performance. Decay, lust and ecstasy live close together. Those who come will become part of the performance themselves. The dress code is your ticket to entry: no effort, no entry.
Renate , doors 21:00 , details ⟨https://www.lunacyberlin.com/⟩
FRI 30
NIGHTCRAWLERS
Photo credit: nightcrawlers
It’s that time of year again, folks, and this extreme full-contact horror experience is back to give you a Halloween scare like no other. In groups of four, you’ll be led through a secret location and subjected to jump scares, waterboarding, restraints, gagging and more. Yes, really. You’ll need to sign a waiver before entering, given a safeword and wear clothes you’re happy to throw away afterwards. If you thought Halloween was all pumpkin carving and fancy dress, think again.
Secret location in Neukölln, details ⟨https://www.instagram.com/nightcrawlers.berlin⟩ .
SAT 31
Lichtenberger Winterzeit
Photo credit: Sabine Gudath
Move aside, spooky season, Mariah Carey is defrosting! And she’s just in time for the first Christmas market of the year. Opening its doors as soon as it can, the Lichtenberger Winterzeit market is an (especially) early opportunity for some festive merriment. Decked out with a wintry Christmas atmosphere, culinary delights and spectacular rides, visitors can expect a winter wonderland full of adventure and fun for the whole family. It’s on through Dec 27.
Landsberger Allee 300, doors 14:00, details ⟨https://lichtenberger-winterzeit.de/⟩
SUN 01
Berlin Science Week
Photo credit: Marc Beckmann
Breaking science out of the institutions and giving it to the people, this week is for boffins of all persuasions. With a 10-day programme that features hundreds of free events in English and German, they’ll transform the city into one big laboratory for conversations with experts, cutting edge research and a deeper look into how science shapes us. Through Nov 10.
Various locations , details ⟨https://berlinscienceweek.com/⟩
