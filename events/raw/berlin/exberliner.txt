What’s On This Week: Our Daily Tips
A curated selection of the best things to do daily in Berlin.
MON 16
Smut Slam
Photo Credit: Smut Slam
Some things you lose, others you find in the most unexpected places and sometimes the search becomes a story. In this edition of Smut Slam, the theme is ‘Lost and Found’. From curious sexual encounters and surprising discoveries to the small and big secrets of desire, it’s all about personal stories. At the storytelling open mic, the brave can share their experiences with the audience and win prizes. Those who prefer to listen are just as welcome. There will also be anonymous confessions and questions from the famous ‘Fuckbucket’.

TUE 17
Goodbye Horses: The Many Lives of Q Lazzarus
Photo credit: Q Lazzarus
Remember ‘Goodbye Horses’, the haunting hit song from The Silence of the Lambs ? Screaming.doc presents Goodbye Horses: The Many Lives of Q Lazzarus , a new film exploring the life of the elusive singer behind the song. Stick around afterwards for a live set from Danny Z, who worked with Q Lazzarus in the 1980s and 90s.
Sputnik Kino , starts 20:00 details ⟨https://www.sputnik-kino.com/film/62454⟩
WED 18
26th Women’s Worlds Film Festival
Photo credit: Still by Laïla Marrakchi’s Strawberries
Filmfest FrauenWelten returns to focus on women’s rights, self-determination and harsh political realities. The festival opens with Laïla Marrakchi’s Strawberries , about two Moroccan women working the strawberry harvest in Spain, where the promise of a better life runs up against exploitation and injustice. Other highlights include Shahrbanoo Sadat’s No Good Men , following Naru (played by Sadat), the only female camera operator at a major television network in Kabul. There’s also Susanne Heinrich’s musical comedy about a mother stuck at home with her boyfriend, Peter Pan, and their newborn: The Miserable Mother . Through Nov 24.
Kino in der Kulturbrauerei , starts 19:00 details ⟨https://filmfest-frauenwelten.de/⟩
THU 19
Sissy Smut Vol 7
Photo credit: Girls In Your Area
A night dedicated to reconnecting, recharging and finding fresh energy, this programme brings music, performance and film from some of Berlin’s most exciting cultural creatives. Reuniting Bleach and Matt Lambert, expect a lineup that packs a punch, featuring trans band Girls In Your Area and Jenna Marvin. After the success of previous editions, we recommend getting your tickets asap as this will definitely sell out.
Volksbühne , starts 19:00 details ⟨https://www.volksbuehne-berlin.de/produktionen/sissy-smut/20261119-1930/⟩
FRI 20
Rejazz Festival
Photo credit: Dovile Sermokas
Unique to Berlin, this jazz festival focuses on female bandleaders and brings inspiring role-models to the stage. Back for its 7th edition, they’ve programmed three days of concerts that include Silke Eberhard, Ariel Bart, Liz Kosack and Aki Takase. If that wasn’t femme-tastic, we don’t know what is. Through Nov 21.
Jazzclub Kunstfabrik Schlot, doors 20:00 details ⟨https://rejazz-festival.de/en/⟩
SAT 21
Zero Waste Future Festival
Photo credit: Wolfgangson
We know Germans are obsessed with separating their trash and, well, it’s all for good reason. Taking place on the European Week of Waste Prevention, this festival highlights the importance of upcycling, recycling and repairing your broken items or unwanted treasures with various workshops, also featuring games, music, snacks and lots of communal fun.
Nochmall, doors 11:00 details ⟨https://www.bsr.de/zwff⟩
SUN 22
Donna The Show
Photo credit: Donna The Show
Arriving back in Berlin following its premiere in 2025 and five performances in Montreal, the updated version of the show features three new dancers, new music and unseen performances. With scenes reminiscent of early 20th Century cabaret, the show mixes projections, spoken word and music, paying homage to a Parisian showgirl review and an edgy Berlin striptease.
