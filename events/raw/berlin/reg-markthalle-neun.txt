Community around food
Markthalle Neun is tangible food culture: marketplace, centre for food production and education, hub for crafts and culture and meeting place for many - in a historical monument in Berlin-Kreuzberg.
Food and people take centre stage here. Bakers bake, brewers brew, butchers butcher. The neighbourhood does daily groceries and guests come from all over Berlin and beyond - for good bread, fresh vegetables and a variety of cuisines. Senior citizens eat together instead of alone, while school classes learn how milk is turned into cheese. This is where community around food lives and grows every day.
