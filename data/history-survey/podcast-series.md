# History podcasts: one place, event or person per episode

Survey for Layered City, 2 Nov 2026. 64 WebSearch calls, no WebFetch. Every episode below showed up in search results with the URL given. Durations come from search snippets only where noted. Otherwise they are typical lengths for the show and should be checked when importing.

Podcast pins in the thinnest cities: Kraków 12, Bucharest 13, Marseille 13, Munich 13, Helsinki 14, Lisbon 14, Zurich 14, Budapest 15, Naples 15.

"Dup?" means a pin already exists at that place, possibly from a different show. Check before importing.

## Ranked by fit

### 1. The Ancients (History Hit). In app: 4 stories
- **Format:** Tristan Hughes interviews an academic or archaeologist. Many episodes cover a single site. About 40–60 min (Everand lists "Rise of Marseilles" at 58 min).
- **Reputation:** High. History Hit is a major UK history network, and guests are mostly university academics.
- **Why it ranks first:** Many episodes are about a single site, and it is clearly under-used at 4 stories.
- **Examples:**
  - Marseille, Old Port / Jardin des Vestiges: *The Rise of Marseilles: France's Oldest City*, https://shows.acast.com/the-ancients/episodes/theriseofmarseilles-francesoldestcity (Dup? Marseille already has two Greek-founding pins at the Old Port.)
  - Athens, Parthenon: *The Parthenon: Wonder of Athens*, https://shows.acast.com/the-ancients/episodes/the-parthenon-wonder-of-athens
  - Athens, Parthenon (earlier, separate episode): *The Parthenon*, https://shows.acast.com/the-ancients/episodes/the-parthenon
  - Rome, Pantheon: *The Pantheon*, https://shows.acast.com/the-ancients/episodes/the-pantheon (Dup? Rome already has a Pantheon pin.)
  - Naples, Herculaneum: *Herculaneum*, https://metacast.app/podcast/the-ancients/iTgFDhDX/herculaneum/OeO5XUtZ (Naples has a Villa of the Papyri pin, but no pin for the town site itself.)
  - Naples, Pompeii: *Pompeii: The Buried City*, https://metacast.app/podcast/the-ancients/iTgFDhDX/pompeii-the-buried-city/HRovsYA8
  - Naples, Pompeii: *Treasures of Pompeii*, https://metacast.app/podcast/the-ancients/iTgFDhDX/treasures-of-pompeii/Wll4x6QB

### 2. Gone Medieval (History Hit). In app: 0 (not in channels.json)
- **Format:** Medievalists Cat Jarman and Matt Lewis interview historians. Some episodes are about one ruler or one site. About 40–50 min (Everand lists "Vlad the Impaler" at 49 min).
- **Reputation:** High. Both hosts are historians.
- **Examples found (fewer per-site episodes surfaced than for The Ancients):**
  - Prague, Charles Bridge / Prague Castle / Karolinum: *Charles IV, Holy Roman Emperor*, https://shows.acast.com/gone-medieval/episodes/charles-iv-holy-roman-emperor
  - Bucharest, Old Princely Court (Curtea Veche): *Vlad the Impaler*, https://shows.acast.com/gone-medieval/episodes/vlad-the-impaler (Dup? Bucharest already has a "Dracula (Vlad the Impaler)" pin at Curtea Veche.)
  - The feed also has general episodes on castles, pilgrimage and stonemasons. These are not tied to one place and don't fit.

### 3. Short History Of... (Noiser). In app: 1 story
- **Format:** Narrated, dramatised documentary episodes. Each one covers a single event, place or person and runs about 45–60 min. Noiser also runs a sister feed, *Ancient Civilisations*.
- **Reputation:** Good. It is professionally produced and uses historian contributors, but it is more dramatised than History Hit.
- **Examples:**
  - Venice: *Venice*, https://pocketcasts.com/podcast/short-history-of/fbecb8e0-d6eb-0139-9b7b-0acc26574db2/venice/8f9933d8-7fed-4b4e-a346-a72460d1679a (covers the whole city; pin at Rialto or San Marco)
  - London, Pudding Lane / Monument: *The Great Fire of London*, https://noiser.com/short-history-of/the-great-fire-of-london
  - Berlin, Berlin Wall Memorial, Bernauer Straße: *The Berlin Wall*, https://noiser.com/short-history-of/the-berlin-wall
  - Amsterdam, Anne Frank House: *Anne Frank*, https://www.noiser.com/short-history-of/anne-frank
  - Istanbul, Hagia Sophia or the walls: *Constantinople*, https://da.player.fm/series/short-history-of-3285834/constantinople
  - Naples, Pompeii: *Pompeii and the Vesuvius Eruption*, https://noiser.com/short-history-of/pompeii-and-the-vesuvius-eruption (also at https://www.noiser.com/ancient-civilisations/pompeii-and-the-vesuvius-eruption)

### 4. HistoryExtra Podcast (BBC History Magazine). In app: 4 stories. History's Greatest Cities (its spin-off): 5
- **Format:** Interviews with historians, about 30–60 min (Everand lists the Vesuvius episode at 39 min). Most episodes are about a theme, but some are about one site or one event.
- **Reputation:** High.
- **Examples:**
  - Naples, Vesuvius: *Why were the Romantics obsessed with Mount Vesuvius?* (John Brewer), https://www.podbean.com/ew/dir-jyrhi-1cced216
  - Naples, Pompeii: *The Final Days of Pompeii*, https://getpodcast.com/podcast/historyextrapodcast/the-final-days-of-pompeii_e14616fd2e
  - Naples, Pompeii: *Before the volcano: life in ancient Pompeii*, https://getpodcast.com/podcast/historyextrapodcast/before-the-volcano-life-in-ancient-pompeii_89b22ba85f
  - Munich, Bürgerbräukeller site: *The Munich Putsch: Hitler's bungled revolution*, https://www.podbean.com/media/share/dir-k6rtf-1bc15430 (Dup? Munich already has a Bürgerbräukeller pin.)
  - History's Greatest Cities has whole-city episodes for **Munich** (https://metacast.app/podcast/history-s-greatest-cities/ei7fRUyw/munich/ncHPNoF9), **Budapest** (https://metacast.app/podcast/history-s-greatest-cities/ei7fRUyw/budapest/MzRenzgR), **Lisbon** (https://podcast.app/lisbon-e342610922), **Prague** and **Kraków**. These are broad city overviews, so they fit less well. The Munich and Kraków episodes may already be in the app: there are "Eight centuries…" and "A historian walks the streets…" pins. Lisbon and Budapest appear not to be.

### 5. Wonders of the World (Caroline Vahrenkamp). In app: 2 stories
- **Format:** An independent show with one monument per episode. Episodes are fairly short (often around 20–30 min) and narrated solo.
- **Reputation:** Medium. It is a careful independent creator, but not an academic one. Sample an episode before bulk-adding.
- **Why it ranks here:** One building per episode is exactly the right format. Only one episode in our cities was confirmed within budget, so the feed needs a scan.
- **Examples:**
  - Lisbon, Jerónimos Monastery: *092 – The Hieronymites Monastery of Lisbon and Vasco da Gama*, https://wonderspodcast.libsyn.com/092-the-hieronymites-monastery-of-lisbon
  - Episode index for scanning: https://wonderspodcast.libsyn.com/

- **Thin-city check:** Most hits in thin cities are already pinned: Munich air disaster, Radio Free Europe, the Imre Nagy reburial and the Budapest 1956 items.
- **Possibly new:**
  - Kraków, Podgórze ghetto / Ghetto Heroes Square: *The Kraków Ghetto* (from the WW2 compilation feed), https://playpodcasts.com/podcasts/witness-history-world-war-two-history/the-krakow-ghetto

### 8. RRI "The History Show" (Radio Romania International, Steliu Lambru). In app: 0
- **Format:** A short weekly English-language radio feature from the state broadcaster. Each one is published as a web article with audio. It often covers one Bucharest building or district.
- **Reputation:** Medium-high. It is the public broadcaster and frequently quotes historians and oral-history archives, but it is not a podcast-native show. Check that audio exists on each page.
- **Why it matters:** Bucharest has the second-thinnest podcast list, and this is the only Bucharest-specific English history series found.
- **Examples:**
  - Bucharest, Uranus district (around the Palace of the Parliament): *The Uranus District*, https://www.rri.ro/en/features-and-reports/the-history-show/the-uranus-district-id131668.html
  - Bucharest, various monuments: *Bucharest's Historical Monuments*, https://www.rri.ro/en/features-and-reports/the-history-show/bucharests-historical-monuments-id129962.html
  - Show archive: https://www.rri.ro/en/features-and-reports/the-history-show/page/3

### 9. Great Lives (BBC Radio 4). In app: 0
- **Format:** A guest champions one historical figure, with an expert alongside. About 28 min.
- **Fit:** Pins would go to the person's house or museum. In thin cities the matches mostly duplicate existing pins.
- **Examples:**
  - Budapest, Semmelweis Museum: *The surgeon Henry Marsh picks… Dr Semmelweis*, https://player.fm/series/great-lives-1301287/the-surgeon-henry-marsh-picks-the-saviour-of-mothers-dr-semmelweis (Dup? Budapest already has a Semmelweis pin.)
  - Budapest, House of Houdini: *Harry Houdini*, https://ontelly.co.uk/programme/great-lives/series-19/harry-houdini (Dup?)

### 10. The Anthill (The Conversation UK). In app: 0
- **Format:** Academics talk on themed miniseries. About 30 min. Reputable.
- **Example:** Lisbon, Baixa / Praça do Comércio: *Recovery part two: Lisbon earthquake*, https://theconversation.com/the-earthquake-that-shocked-europe-how-lisbon-recovered-after-1755-recovery-podcast-series-part-two-140358 (Lisbon's existing earthquake pin is at São Domingos, so the Baixa rebuild is a different spot.)

## City-specific shows found (lower confidence)

- **Helsinki:** The City of Helsinki has an official podcast, *The Stories and Conversations from Helsinki*: https://www.hel.fi/en/culture-and-leisure/the-stories-and-conversations-from-helsinki-podcast. No episode titles surfaced, so it needs a look.
- **Zurich:** *Switzerland, a Chronicle* (Peter Walker), https://shows.acast.com/switzerland-a-chronicle/episodes. It tells Swiss history in chronological order, and no Zurich-specific episode was confirmed. *The Swiss Connection* (SWI swissinfo.ch) is mainly current affairs and science. Zurich remains the hardest gap.
- **Budapest:** *Hungarian History Podcast* (Gábor Szántai), https://www.iheart.com/podcast/1333-hungarian-history-podcast-274534400. It is a chronological narrative show, so it fits poorly.
- **Kraków:** *Kraków Stories* (https://krakowstories.buzzsprout.com). Authorship and reputation could not be checked. *Multicultural Centre of Kraków* has a Kazimierz audioguide, which is closer to a tour than a podcast.
- **Marseille:** No reputable single-site English history episode was found beyond The Ancients. Searches for Le Panier in 1943 and the Unité d'habitation turned up only magazine articles and low-signal architecture feeds.
- **Naples:** *A History of Italy* podcast (1 story in app) has a narrative episode, *202 – Spanish Italy in the early 1500s – The Kingdom of Naples*, which is too broad. No Masaniello episode was found.
- **Munich:** The NS-Dokumentationszentrum has digital and audio offerings (https://www.nsdoku.de/en/digital/discover), but no English podcast series was confirmed.

## Checked and not recommended

- **Fall of Civilizations, Hardcore History, Revolutions:** episodes run for hours and cover whole civilisations or revolutions, not one site.
- **Our Fake History:** reputable, but no episode tied to one of our cities surfaced.
- **Real Dictators (Noiser):** the show exists, but no episode-level results came back for Ceaușescu or Salazar.
- **Fexingo History** ("The Story of Switzerland / Finland…"): looks like mass-produced, possibly AI-generated feeds. Note that "The Story of Switzerland: Neutrality, Wealth, and Hidden Power" is already in channels.json with 1 story, so it is worth reviewing.
- **BBC The Long View:** not searched in depth. It is built on present/past comparisons, not places.
- **Radio Prague (Czechast, 6), RTÉ (Documentary on One 1, RTÉ Archives 2):** not pursued because Prague and Dublin are not thin.
