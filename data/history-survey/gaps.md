# History coverage audit: the places travellers ask about, checked against the pins

How this was done: for each city I listed the 10 to 13 historic places and events a curious visitor would most expect to have explained. I used my own knowledge for this and made no web searches. I then checked each one against `pins/<city>.json`. The first pass was a keyword match on title and place. After that I read every non-artwork podcast and video pin (about 1,450) by hand, to catch stories whose titles don't name the event. One example is Prague's "Three men thrown from a window ignite a European war", which is the 1618 Defenestration.

Status key:
- **covered**: at least one podcast or video pin is clearly about it
- **thin**: only a book, film, song or general VoiceMap/Rick Steves audiotour touches it, or a P/V pin is there but about something else (for example a craft or lifestyle video at the site)
- **missing**: nothing pinned

Day-trip sites (Dachau, Waterloo, Pompeii, El Escorial, Þingvellir) are counted because the app already pins other day trips.

## Overall summary

### Weakest cities for history (most "thin/missing" among the obvious sights)
1. **Amsterdam**: Anne Frank House, the Jewish Quarter and the Royal Palace have no P/V. Most of its 69 P/V pins are lifestyle and design videos.
2. **Copenhagen**: almost nothing on Christian IV's city, absolutism, the fires or colonialism. Its P/V skews to food and design.
3. **Madrid**: the Royal Palace, Dos de Mayo 1808, Plaza Mayor and Habsburg Madrid are all missing. Its P/V is heavy on food and bullfighting.
4. **London**: big but uneven. The Tower, the Abbey, the Gunpowder Plot, Charles I's execution, the Plague and slavery are all thin or missing despite 317 P/V pins.
5. **Naples**: San Gennaro, Masaniello and the Bourbons are all missing.
6. **Bucharest**, **Belgrade**, **Budapest**: good on the 20th century, weak before 1900 and weak on the Holocaust.
7. **Athens**: classical sites are saturated (Smarthistory), but there is nothing on the WWII occupation and famine, Byzantine and Ottoman Athens, or 1896.

**Strongest:** Venice, Istanbul, Florence, Stockholm, Edinburgh, Prague, Rome (ancient only), Manchester, Marseille, Seville.

### Kinds of history that are missing across cities
- **The Holocaust and Nazi persecution at the actual sites.** This is the clearest gap. These sites usually have only a book or a feature film: Anne Frank House, Berlin's Holocaust Memorial, Topography of Terror and Kristallnacht, the Vel d'Hiv, the Rome Ghetto raid and Fosse Ardeatine, Dachau, Budapest's Shoes on the Danube and Wallenberg, Vienna's Judenplatz, the Kraków ghetto, Belgrade's Sajmište, the Bucharest 1941 pogrom and Athens under occupation. Heydrich in Prague also has only a book and a film.
- **Medieval and early-modern religious violence:** St Bartholomew's Day (Paris), Hus and the Hussites (Prague), the 1391 pogroms (Seville, Barcelona), the 1506 massacre (Lisbon), the Reformation in Edinburgh, and the Inquisition at Plaza Mayor and Rossio.
- **Royal palaces and dynastic set-pieces that every visitor walks past:** the Tower of London, Westminster Abbey coronations, the Royal Palace and Habsburg Madrid, Naples' Palazzo Reale, Amsterdam's Dam palace, Amalienborg and Christian IV's Copenhagen, Stockholm's Tre Kronor and Gustav Vasa.
- **Ottoman Europe outside Istanbul:** Ottoman Buda, Ottoman Belgrade, Ottoman Athens. Only the sieges are told.
- **Colonialism and slavery outside Lisbon and Brussels:** London's docks, Copenhagen and the Danish West Indies, the Dutch WIC and Keti Koti in Amsterdam, colonial Marseille.
- **19th-century nation-building and revolutions:** the Risorgimento (Rome, Venice 1848), Madrid 1808, Vienna 1848, Norway 1814 and 1905, Iceland 1944, Reykjavík's Alþingi, Waterloo.
- **Fascism in Italy and Spain:** Mussolini's Rome, the 1936 coup in Seville, the 1938 Barcelona bombings.

Well covered already: the communist era (Berlin, Budapest, Prague, Bucharest, Belgrade, Kraków), Cold War episodes, ancient Greece and Rome (art-object videos), the Renaissance (Florence, Venice), and late-20th-century events (Palme, 23-F, 11-M, the IRA bomb, 1989).

### Implication for sourcing
The missing items are mostly single-event, single-spot stories, which suits one-episode-per-topic series. The Holocaust gap and the "royal palace" gap would each fill most of the weakest cities at once.
