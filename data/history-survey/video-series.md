# History video series: gap survey

Scope: video series that are free to watch, made by a reputable producer, and where each video covers one place, building or event in one of our 32 cities. I used 62 WebSearch calls and no WebFetch.

How to read the URLs: every URL below appeared in a search result. The search results often showed a YouTube title and URL without naming the channel. Where that happened I mark the item **(channel unconfirmed)**. Check the uploader before importing. Rights for full TV documentaries reuploaded to YouTube are often unclear, so those are flagged too.

"In app" means the channel is in channels.json, with its story count.

## 1. RTÉ Archives (Ireland's national broadcaster)
- **Format:** Short archive TV and newsreel clips, each about one event, with an editorial write-up. There are also curated "exhibitions", such as Easter 1916 told through veterans' interviews. Free on rte.ie.
- **Reputation:** National public broadcaster's archive.
- **Length:** 1–10 min.
- **In app:** No.
- **Examples (Dublin):**
  - Demolition of Nelson's Pillar, 1966 (O'Connell St): https://www.rte.ie/archives/2022/1122/375076-demolition-of-nelsons-pillar/
  - Remains of Nelson's Pillar removed (O'Connell St): https://www.rte.ie/archives/2015/0706/712966-remains-of-nelsons-pillar/
  - "Take The Post Office", Portraits 1916, Fergus O'Kelly (GPO): https://www.rte.ie/archives/exhibitions/1993-easter-1916/portraits-1916/765619-portraits-1916-fergus-okelly
  - "I Seen Men Crying With The Surrender", Portraits 1916, Tom Kearney: https://www.rte.ie/archives/exhibitions/1993-easter-1916/portraits-1916/767287-portraits-1916-tom-kearney
  - Aiséirí pageant at Croke Park, 1966 (Croke Park): https://www.rte.ie/archives/exhibitions/1993-easter-1916/2769-events-of-easter-week-1966-in-colour/689570-aiseiri-glor-reim-na-casca-croke-park
  - Easter Rising 1916: https://www.rte.ie/archives/2014/0424/610615-easter-rising-1916
- **Note:** Dublin has 17 video pins, and the GPO has a book, a film and a podcast but no video. The Irish Film Institute's free IFI Archive Player is a sister source with place tags, for example https://ifiarchiveplayer.ie/tag/eden-quay/.

## 2. INA (Institut national de l'audiovisuel)
- **Format:** French TV and newsreel archive clips, one event each. Free on ina.fr. **Fresques** are regional collections with maps; "Sudorama" covers the south of France since 1940.
- **Reputation:** French state audiovisual archive.
- **Length:** 1–5 min.
- **In app:** No.
- **Language:** French.
- **Examples:**
  - Marseille, Vieux-Port: "La démolition du quartier du vieux port" https://m.ina.fr/video/AFE86002529/la-demolition-du-quartier-du-vieux-port-video.html
  - Marseille, Vieux-Port: "Evacuation du quartier du Vieux Port à Marseille" (Sudorama) https://fresques.ina.fr/sudorama/fiche-media/00000000064/evacuation-du-quartier-du-vieux-port-a-marseille.html
  - Marseille, the same event on YouTube: "La démolition du quartier du vieux port" https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3_za2Ebl7F4 **(channel unconfirmed)**
  - Marseille: "Rafle, évacuation et destruction des quartiers du Vieux-Port en 1943" https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ywsXTY7njfc **(channel unconfirmed)**
  - Paris, Les Halles: destruction of the Baltard pavilions. This is on INA's licensing site (mediaclip), not the free site; look for the same clip on ina.fr. https://mediaclip.ina.fr/en/i09163713-destruction-of-the-baltard-pavilions.html
- **Note:** Marseille has only 8 video pins, and the 1943 Vieux-Port round-up and demolition has no pin. Sudorama could give Marseille 10 or more pins on its own.

## 3. Rick Steves' Classroom Europe
- **Format:** Short clips cut from the TV series, one place or topic each, many of them history. Free at classroom.ricksteves.com.
- **Reputation:** PBS series. Rick Steves Europe is already the app's second-largest video channel.
- **Length:** 1–5 min.
- **In app:** "Classroom Europe" has 3 stories; "Rick Steves Europe" has 103.
- **Examples:**
  - Bucharest, Palace of the Parliament: "Bucharest and Ceaușescu's Massive Palace" https://classroom.ricksteves.com/videos/bucharest-and-ceau-escu-s-massive-palace
  - Oslo, City Hall and Norway's Resistance Museum (Akershus): "Oslo: City Hall and Resistance Museum" https://classroom.ricksteves.com/videos/oslo-city-hall-and-resistance-museum
  - Kraków playlist: https://classroom.ricksteves.com/playlists/z93q2
  - Prague, Charles Bridge (PBS clip): https://www.pbssocal.org/clip/rick-steves-europe-prague-czech-republic-charles-bridge-and-czech-language
- **Note:** Not a new producer, but the clip-level catalogue looks under-mined for the thin cities. The PBS episode pages found cover Kraków, Helsinki, Stockholm and Oslo.

## 4. toldinstone (Garrett Ryan, PhD classicist)
- **Format:** Tours of single buildings, museums and sites, plus thematic videos. The tours are listed on his site under "Building and Museum Visits", "Site and City Tours" and "Guided Virtual Tours".
- **Reputation:** Well regarded, written by an academic, with sources given.
- **Length:** 10–25 min.
- **In app:** 2 stories.
- **Examples:**
  - Rome, Pantheon: "A Historical Tour of Rome's Pantheon" https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EDeQkttORoc
  - Rome, Trajan's Column: "The Strangest Scenes on Trajan's Column" https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=O18-tmPH13s
  - Istanbul, Hagia Sophia: "How Hagia Sophia Went From Church to Mosque" https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s3Nt5xmp7Ng
  - Istanbul, Hagia Sophia, the same tour on his site: https://toldinstone.com/a-historical-tour-of-hagia-sophia/
  - Rome, Ostia Antica: "Ostia: the Port of Ancient Rome" https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LK6c7bP-ttg **(appeared in a toldinstone search; channel not shown)**
  - Index pages to mine for more: https://toldinstone.com/building-and-museum-visits/ and https://toldinstone.com/site-and-city-tours/
- **Note:** The Pantheon, Trajan's Column and Hagia Sophia already have Smarthistory video pins, so these would add a second take rather than new places. Ostia has no video pin. The index pages probably include Athens and the Naples Archaeological Museum, but no search result confirmed a URL for either.

## 5. National newsreel and TV archives (portals, not YouTube channels)
Each clip covers one event, and all are free. Most are not in English, so they need English pin text.

- **Archivio Luce (Istituto Luce, Italy)** covers Rome, Naples, Venice and Florence.
  - Naples: "Napoli acclama re Umberto" https://patrimonio.archivioluce.com/luce-web/detail/IL3000082780/1/-2980.html
  - Luce also runs a large YouTube channel; I didn't confirm specific uploads.
- **Filmhíradók Online (Hungarian National Film Archive newsreels)** covers Budapest. English interface: https://filmhiradokonline.hu/en.php
- **Schweizer Filmwochenschau / Ciné-Journal suisse (Swiss Federal Archives)** covers Zurich, 1940–75, online. Entry points: https://www.bar.admin.ch/en/film-and-television and https://applab.bar.admin.ch/applications/archipanion-schweizer-filmwochenschau
- **Filmarkivet.se (Swedish Film Institute and National Library)** covers Stockholm. https://www.filminstitutet.se/en/learn-more-about-film/digitisation/other-fields-of-use-of-our-film-collections/filmarkivet.se/
- **TVR (Romanian public TV) on Google Arts & Culture** covers Bucharest. It includes 1989 Revolution footage and stills from TVR headquarters, Revolution Square and the Central Committee building.
  - "1989 Revolution of Romanian National Television" https://artsandculture.google.com/asset/1989-revolution-of-romanian-national-television/ZQF78GZxLWaadQ
  - "People in the Revolution Square" https://artsandculture.google.com/asset/people-in-the-revolution-square/7wGduUsWquxNzA
  - "Bucharest, city of victory" story: https://artsandculture.google.com/story/bucharest-city-of-victory-tvr-the-romanian-public-broadcaster/YgXxxZahpwQA8A?hl=en
  - Many of these are stills, so check which items are video.
- **BFI Player "Britain on Film"** covers Manchester, Edinburgh and London. It is a free archive with a map. A collection example: https://player.bfi.org.uk/free/collection/storytrails-the-peoples-map. Coverage of Manchester's archive films: https://confidentials.com/manchester/watch-forgotten-manchester-archive-footage
- **EUscreen** pools TV clips from many European broadcasters (https://euscreen.eu/about/). It could help Belgrade, Brussels and Bucharest, but no specific clips surfaced.

## 6. BBC Archive
- **Format:** Clips from old BBC programmes titled "YEAR: TITLE", usually about one place or event.
- **Reputation:** BBC.
- **Length:** 3–15 min.
- **In app:** 2 stories.
- **Examples:**
  - Manchester, the Town Hall / city corporation: "1974: Manchester - the Corporation and the People" https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=C0SOmCfu6YE **(the title matches BBC Archive's style; channel unconfirmed)**
- **Note:** I didn't use more searches here. The channel is large and worth browsing for Manchester and Edinburgh by hand.

## 7. Mark Felton Productions
- **Format:** WWII history, often tied to a specific building.
- **Reputation:** PhD historian, popular. Fine for WWII sites.
- **Length:** 10–20 min.
- **In app:** 8 stories.
- **Examples (all channel unconfirmed in the results):**
  - Munich, Königsplatz "Temples of Honour": "Nazi Temples of Doom - Munich 1945" https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2vBZ30I2M0s
  - Munich, Prinzregentenplatz 16: "Capturing Hitler's Apartment" https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y8sndwh3eds
  - Munich, likely the Führerbau: "Finding Hitler's Forgotten Office" https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XHAMZmno1L8
  - Prague, Church of Sts Cyril and Methodius: "Operation Anthropoid: The Final Moments of Heydrich's Assassins (NO AI)" https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8iz8YS2bqzM. The "(NO AI)" in the title is unlike Felton's style, so this may be another channel. Verify it.
- **Note:** Prague's Cyril and Methodius church has a book and a film but no video. Munich has no Königsplatz or Feldherrnhalle video pins.

## 10. Rome reconstructions: Ancient Rome Live and Flyover Zone
- **Ancient Rome Live** (AIRC, Darius Arya): one-monument videos with articles. Examples: https://ancientromelive.org/baths-of-titus/, https://ancientromelive.org/baths-of-trajan/, https://ancientromelive.org/the-greatest-domes-of-ancient-rome/, and the index https://ancientromelive.org/index/entries/monuments/

## 12. History Hit
- Its on-location documentaries are mostly on the paid History Hit TV service, for example "The History of Westminster Abbey": https://access.historyhit.com/dan-snow/season:1/videos/the-history-of-westminster-abbey
- The free YouTube channel has some on-location videos. Example: "Dan Snow Uncovers The Secrets Of Westminster Abbey" https://m.youtube.com/watch?v=ALN5kwGkymQ
- Dan Snow's History Hit podcast has one-place episodes: Edinburgh Castle, the Tower of London, the Colosseum and the Acropolis (for example https://shows.acast.com/dansnowshistoryhit/episodes/the-acropolis). That belongs in the podcast survey.

## 14. Not recommended
- **Fall of Civilizations:** 2–4 h episodes about whole civilisations, for example "Byzantium – Last of the Romans", YouTube ID 2JHCfe86A8U per https://rosetta.to/u/fallofcivilizations/11-byzantium-last-of-the-romans-part-1-of-2. Not tied to one place.
- **Historia Civilis:** Roman Republic narrative. My search only found reaction videos, not the original.

## Gaps I could not fill
- I found no reputable English-language history video series for **Belgrade**, **Seville** or **Zurich** beyond the archive portals above.
- NRK, ARD/BR, Yle, RÚV, RTS and RTVE NO-DO all have archives, but searches returned no usable per-clip URLs. RTVE has made 6,700+ NO-DO newsreels public, which could matter for Seville and Madrid.
- DW Documentary / DW History: none of my searches returned a confirmed DW history video.
- "München Now & Then" (Feldherrnhalle, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GLFCnGKfhCE) is a place-by-place Munich series, but I couldn't identify who makes it.
